Xinlong Du

I'm a PhD student in Industrial Engineering at Purdue University, working in the Stochastic System Lab led by Prof. Harsha Honnappa. Previously, I studied physics at Reed College and statistics at Purdue University.

I'm mostly interested in solving inference problems in stochastic systems using modern machine learning techniques, but I also enjoy thinking about problems in game theory, reinforcement learning, and differential privacy.

Currently, I'm working on temporal point process modeling and in-context reinforcement learning.
