2024-25 / R package

SimBaRepro

Simulation-based, finite-sample inference for privatized data

CRAN Paper Back to projects

In 2024-25, I worked with Prof. Jordan Awan and Zhanyu Wang to turn their research on simulation-based reproducibility inference into an R package published on CRAN.

The package uses simulation-based methods to obtain p-values for hypothesis tests, confidence intervals, and multivariate confidence regions from privatized data. The multivariate regions can also be projected down to two-dimensional confidence regions for visualization.

The package page on CRAN has installation details, documentation, and the current released source.
